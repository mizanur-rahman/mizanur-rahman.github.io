I am a Lead Data Scientist at Royal Bank of Canada (RBC) with over 7 years of experience in machine learning and data science, and an ML researcher at York University, where I conduct research at the Intelligent Visualization Lab.

In industry, I work across the full machine learning lifecycle, from business problem formulation and stakeholder collaboration to model development, deployment, evaluation, and monitoring. More recently, I have been focusing on multi-agent AI systems for financial applications.

My research focuses on data science agents, vision-language models for data visualization, chart understanding, model evaluation, and LLM post-training. I am particularly interested in building data science agents that can interact with real computer environments and autonomously complete end-to-end data science workflows.
